What did she send?

Copy her message in WhatsApp, then tap Paste. You can also share text to Wingman from other apps.

Quick rules that always work
  • Match her energy and length. Don’t send paragraphs to a one-liner.
  • Ask one real question about her, then share something about you.
  • Suggest a specific, low-pressure plan: “coffee Thursday?” beats “we should hang out”.
  • Don’t double-text in a panic. Give her room to reply.
  • If she’s unsure or says no, be gracious and move on. That’s attractive too.
